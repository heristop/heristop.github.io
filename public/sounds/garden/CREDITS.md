# Garden sound effects

Both files are adaptations of CC0 assets (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

- `gardener-hit.mp3`: Kenney, Impact Sounds, `impactWood_medium_000.ogg`. Trimmed and faded.
  https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
- `card-reveal.mp3`: Kenney, Casino Audio, `card-slide-1.ogg`, mixed with JaggedStone, Magic Spell SFX, `magical_3.ogg`. Adjusted levels, delayed magic by 130 ms, faded and converted to mono MP3.
  https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio
  https://opengameart.org/content/magic-spell-sfx

Downloaded 2026-09-08. Attribution is optional under CC0; sources are retained here for provenance.

## Background music

- `zen-garden.mp3`: "Revelation" by Indieteur, CC0.
  https://opengameart.org/content/revelation
  Source: `Revelation_0.mp3`. Converted to 96 kbps MP3, with gentle opening and closing fades for repeated playback. The mix is attenuated to 16% (-15.92 dB), so its quiet level also works on iOS without relying on media element volume. Downloaded 2026-09-08.

## Gate arrival

- `gate-bowl.mp3`: "Gong Bell (monkay's Singing Bowl [modified])" by qubodup, based on "SingingBowl.wav" by Monkay. CC0.
  https://freesound.org/people/qubodup/sounds/169289/
  https://freesound.org/people/Monkay/sounds/48325/
  Source: Freesound public HQ MP3 preview. First six seconds, low-pass at 3200 Hz, softened attack and 3.2-second fade, mono 96 kbps MP3. Downloaded 2026-09-08.
